# ContactBlock

The page's closing ask, sitting between Projects and the Footer. A heading, one line of context about what you're open to, and the actions — one `btn-primary` plus the secondary routes, with the email address also shown in plain text so it can be copied without opening a mail client.

The consumer provides: the heading, the one-line context (say what kind of work, and your availability, in the same sentence), the primary action, and the plain-text address.

Do:
- Say what happens next ("I reply within a day") rather than "Get in touch" — the block converts on specificity.
- Show the address in text as well as behind the button; plenty of people won't click a `mailto:`.
- Keep this and the Hero's links in sync; if the hero offers a résumé, so does this.

Don't:
- Don't put a contact form here unless something actually receives it — a form that silently fails is worse than no form.
- Don't list every social account; two or three routes, the ones you actually check.
