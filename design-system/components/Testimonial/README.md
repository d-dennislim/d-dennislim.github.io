# Testimonial

A client or colleague's words in `quote`, with attribution beneath in `body-sm` / `ink-muted`. The `accent` left edge is the only place in this system a coloured border-accent is allowed — it marks speech that isn't yours.

The consumer provides: the quote (their words, unedited beyond trimming), the person's name, their role, and their organisation if they've agreed to it being named.

Do:
- Get written permission before publishing a name and employer, and honour the level they agreed to — "Engineering Manager, retail client" is a legitimate attribution when a name isn't cleared.
- Quote the specific sentence, not the whole paragraph. One claim per testimonial.
- Place at most two on the page; a wall of praise reads as a landing page, not an engineer's site.

Don't:
- Don't write, paraphrase, or "tidy" a testimonial into something the person didn't say, and never publish an anonymous quote you composed yourself.
- Don't use this for a certification or an award — those are the Certifications shelf.
