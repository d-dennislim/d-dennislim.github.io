# ArticleItem

One row in a writing list: title on the left as a link, date and where it was published on the right. Used for a Writing section on the portfolio, and for the "more from me" list at the foot of a case study.

The consumer provides: title, URL, publication date, and the venue if it was published somewhere other than your own site.

Do:
- Link to the canonical location — if it ran on a client blog or a community site, link there and name the venue, rather than re-hosting it silently.
- Order newest first, and cap the visible list at five with a link to the full archive.
- Write titles that name the thing solved, not the technology alone ("Cutting a Fargate bill in half without touching the application" beats "Fargate cost notes").

Don't:
- Don't list drafts, or posts you plan to write.
- Don't pad the list with reposts of other people's work; this section is evidence that you can explain things.
