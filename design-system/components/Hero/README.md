# Hero

The About section: an asymmetric two-up. Left is identity — eyebrow, name (`display`, the only place it appears), role, a 2–3 sentence bio, status line, and 2–4 links. Right is a `console-panel` — a small "$ whoami" card holding the avatar initials and three quick stats. It's the one place on the page allowed to feel like a dashboard widget instead of a document; everywhere else stays plain text.

The consumer provides: initials for the avatar (2 characters max), name, role, bio, the link list, and up to three short stat pairs (a number and a one-word label) — years of experience, certification count, a real headline metric from Experience. Never invent a stat; if you don't have three real ones, show two.

Do:
- Put the role in `accent`, not `brand` — `brand` stays reserved for actionable things.
- Keep the console stats to numbers you can defend in an interview; this panel is a preview of the proof, not a claim on its own.

Don't:
- Don't stretch the bio past 3 sentences.
- Don't turn the console panel into a second bio — labels and numbers only, no prose inside it.
